# Security Audit — Botium Toys (Fictional Case Study)

## Overview
Conducted a full internal IT security audit for Botium Toys, a fictional 
small U.S.-based toy company, as part of the Google Cybersecurity 
Professional Certificate program. The audit assessed the company's security 
posture, identified risks, and evaluated compliance with industry frameworks.

## Objectives
- Review and assess the company's existing IT asset management practices
- Identify gaps in security controls across people, technology, and processes
- Evaluate compliance with NIST CSF, PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC standards
- Provide actionable recommendations to reduce risk exposure

## Frameworks & Standards Applied
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)
- PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation — EU)
- SOC 1 & SOC 2

## What I Did
1. Reviewed the audit scope, goals, and risk assessment report
2. Analysed assets currently managed by the IT department
3. Completed a Controls and Compliance Checklist covering:
   - Administrative/Managerial controls
   - Technical controls
   - Physical/Operational controls
4. Identified which compliance best practices were and were not in place
5. Drafted recommendations for the IT manager

## Key Findings (Summary)
- **Least Privilege** and **Separation of Duties** were not implemented — 
  all employees had access to sensitive customer and card data
- **No Intrusion Detection System (IDS)** was in place
- **No encryption** on customer credit card information — a direct PCI DSS 
  and GDPR violation
- **No password management system** despite a weak password policy
- **Physical controls** such as CCTV, locks, and fire suppression were in 
  place and adequate
- Recommended immediate remediation for access controls, encryption, IDS 
  deployment, and a formal disaster recovery plan
